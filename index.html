Time Series · Statistical Modeling · Climate

Atmospheric CO₂ Forecasting — Mauna Loa Record

Can 67 years of climate data forecast future CO₂ levels accurately enough to inform policy?

RARIMASARIMATime SeriesRegressionClimate Data

Analyzed NOAA's Mauna Loa Observatory dataset (1958–2024) using R. Compared linear regression, ARIMA, and SARIMA models evaluated by RMSE and AIC. Produced a formal research paper and a general-audience presentation.

Key Finding
SARIMA captured long-run trend and seasonal oscillations with the lowest forecast error. Projections indicate concentrations crossing policy-relevant thresholds within the decade — quantitative evidence for near-term emissions targets.
