Atmospheric CO₂ Forecasting — Mauna Loa Record

Can 67 years of climate data forecast CO₂ levels accurately enough to inform policy?

RARIMASARIMATime SeriesRegression

Analyzed NOAA's Mauna Loa Observatory dataset (1958–2024) in R. Compared linear regression, ARIMA, and SARIMA models by RMSE and AIC. Produced a formal research paper and general-audience presentation.

Key Finding
SARIMA captured long-run trend and seasonal oscillations with the lowest forecast error. Projections cross policy-relevant thresholds within the decade — quantitative evidence for near-term emissions targets.
