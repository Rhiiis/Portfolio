Atmospheric CO₂ Forecasting — Mauna Loa Record
Can 67 years of climate data forecast future CO₂ levels accurately enough to inform policy?
RARIMASARIMATime SeriesRegressionClimate Data
→

Analyzed NOAA's Mauna Loa Observatory dataset spanning 1958–2024 using R. Compared linear regression, ARIMA, and SARIMA models; evaluated via RMSE and AIC to select the best-fit forecasting approach. Produced both a formal research paper and a presentation communicating findings to a general audience.

Key Findings & So What
SARIMA captured both long-run upward trend and seasonal oscillations with the lowest forecast error. Projections indicate concentrations crossing policy-relevant thresholds within the decade — quantitative evidence that strengthens the case for near-term emissions targets.
